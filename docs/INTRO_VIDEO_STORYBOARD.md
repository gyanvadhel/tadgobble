# tadgobble intro — approved production storyboard

Status: approved by the user, including “Gobblers” for makers. Production uses isolated local demo data.

Concept: **A better rabbit hole.**

Format: approximately 40 seconds, 9:16 vertical for Instagram Reels, planned 1080 × 1920 / 60 fps delivery.

The problem: useful products get buried in noisy feeds and scattered saved links; makers need a place for their products to be discovered. The primary conversion is visiting tadgobble to explore, with a secondary invitation to submit a product. Avoid promises of traffic or sales.

Three visual anchors, verified against the current code:

1. **Gobble-powered discovery:** the purple blob eats a product ticket when Feed me a find is clicked, then opens the product preview.
2. **Explore and stash:** colorful expanding product drawers, actual website covers, category filters, and persistent saved finds.
3. **The launch kitchen:** four-step maker submission with a live product ticket, automatic website capture, audience/category choices, colors, and a review receipt.

## Edit and sound direction

- Confident, conversational English voice: playful and slightly deadpan on the jokes. Pronounce the name TAD-gobble.
- Bouncy electronic percussion with click, chomp, and soft impact accents. Keep the voice clearly above the music.
- Snap zooms of roughly 1.8–2.4×, eased cursor arcs, whip pans, and short speed ramps between actions. Slow down at the moment each feature activates.
- Use 4–8° perspective tilts and layered shadows during transitions; settle the UI when viewers need to read it.
- Add motion blur to fast movement; keep held UI and captions crisp.
- Center-bottom captions in short 2–4-word groups. Each new word springs in on its spoken beat, with butter/plum contrast and tangerine emphasis. Keep them above Instagram's overlay area and away from the clicked control.
- Preserve the original purple, lobed Gobble throughout. Use the site's butter, plum, lavender, and tangerine palette and Bricolage Grotesque display type.
