# Tadgobble launch poster

Create one downloadable portrait poster for the founder's LinkedIn launch,
also usable on Instagram. Invite the first creators without inventing users,
submissions, traffic, or results. No publishing is requested.

## Design plan

- Butter `#f9f4a7`: main canvas, warm and immediately recognisable.
- Plum `#39245a`: headline, mascot, and invitation panel.
- Lavender `#c5b5f5`: the shared stage for creative work.
- Paper `#fffef6`: sketch sheet and the mascot's eyes.
- Orange `#ff7547`: one illustrated flower and a small design detail.

Bricolage Grotesque carries large lowercase display text and the wordmark.
DM Sans carries the short description, artwork labels, and readable URL.
Use the exact original, tailless Gobble path from the application.

Concept A: a bold typographic invitation above a little gallery, with Gobble
as its first witness. Left aligned type, loose overlapping original artwork,
and a quiet, full-width invitation below.

```text
  [Gobble] tadgobble.                 made it?
                                    feed the gobble.
  ur work
  deserves
  witnesses.

  A free showcase for art, design & tiny apps.

       [sketch] [design]     [Gobble looking over]
              [tiny app]        let him see it.

  [  share what you made.                  ]
  [  tadgobble.web.app        free to share ]
```

Concept B: a product dashboard in a laptop mockup. Reject it because the app
also welcomes offline art and people with no portfolio or website.

Review before building: keep A, but omit extra sticker badges, faux member
counts, and ornamental browser frames around the whole composition. The tiny
app illustration can have a window bar because it identifies that medium.
The hero is the unusually large headline; all decorative detail stays in the
gallery. This uses the actual brand rather than a generic software launch kit.
Illustrations are original examples for this poster, not claimed submissions.

Deliver 1080 × 1350 PNG and JPEG, a 2× PNG master, a self-contained editable
HTML, and short posting copy with alt text. Inspect the exported image at full
size and at phone-feed scale. Check for cut-off text and export dimensions.
