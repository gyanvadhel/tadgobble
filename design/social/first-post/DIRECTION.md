# Tadgobble — first Instagram post

Make one three-slide introduction that sounds like its founder: informal,
chronically online, welcoming to someone who only has a photo of a painting.
Deliver a finished carousel, caption, and alt text. Creation only; do not publish.

## Design plan

Palette: butter `#f9f4a7`, plum `#39245a`, lavender `#c5b5f5`, paper
`#fffef6`, and orange `#ff7547`. These come from the existing brand.
Bricolage Grotesque carries the oversized, lowercase headlines; DM Sans handles
short supporting lines. Reuse the exact original tailless Gobble silhouette.

Concept A: oversized type, Gobble, and a few original paper illustrations.

```text
1 / hook                2 / what belongs        3 / invitation
tadgobble.              tadgobble.              tadgobble.
you                     ur work                no portfolio?
cooked.                 deserves               no problem.
                        witnesses.
we brought the          [sketch] [design]       photo + title
chronically  [Gobble]        [tiny app]          send for review
online.                                        [Gobble]
meet tadgobble / swipe   art, design, side quests share your work / URL
```

Concept B: screenshots inside decorative browser frames. Reject this for the
first post: it looks like a software launch and excludes people with offline art.

Review: A connects the food joke to the existing mascot and gives the reader a
clear next action. Use big left-aligned text, a single visual gesture per slide,
and roomy margins. No invented member counts, testimonials, artist submissions,
or traffic promises. Illustrations are made for this post, not presented as real
community work. Copy should be legible at phone-feed scale.

Export: three 1080 × 1350 PNGs and JPEGs, a contact sheet, editable standalone
HTML, a caption, and descriptive alt text. Check each finished image visually.
